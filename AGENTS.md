# JustDump working agreement

This agreement applies to people and coding agents working in this repository.

1. Read `README.md`, `docs/product-requirements.md`, `docs/progress.md`, and the other documents relevant to the milestone before starting work.
2. Deliver the agreed JustDump v1 continuously across the roadmap rather than pausing for approval after every milestone. Keep work ordered in small, demonstrable checkpoints.
3. Before starting the continuous delivery run, ask only the owner decisions that materially affect cost, privacy, hosting, deployment, or product behaviour. Explain the recommendation and trade-off, then proceed autonomously with routine technical decisions.
4. Within the authorised delivery scope, proceed with routine edits, migrations, tests, installations, and fixes without repeatedly asking for permission. Stop only for an external blocker, a new material decision, or a requested review.
5. Explain important code and give the owner hands-on steps to verify the result.
6. Update `docs/progress.md`, record what was learned, and record significant decisions with a status of confirmed, proposed, or unresolved. Do not describe planned or unverified behaviour as working.
7. Continue through the agreed v1 delivery sequence until the application is demonstrably usable on the owner's physical device, then pause for final acceptance. Do not expand beyond the authorised v1 scope.
8. Never commit credentials, tokens, signing keys, private configuration, or the owner's personal content.
9. Do not deploy, incur costs, enable paid services, or push changes to a remote without explicit permission. A one-time recorded authorisation may cover the agreed v1 delivery; never force-push.
10. Keep documentation consistent with behaviour that has actually been verified.
