# JustDump working agreement

This agreement applies to people and coding agents working in this repository.

1. Read `README.md`, `docs/product-requirements.md`, `docs/progress.md`, and the other documents relevant to the milestone before starting work.
2. Work on one agreed milestone from `docs/roadmap.md` at a time.
3. Before a new milestone, explain the relevant concept, viable options, trade-offs, and a recommendation. Ask the owner to agree before implementation begins.
4. Within an agreed milestone, proceed with routine edits and proportionate checks without repeatedly asking for permission.
5. Explain important code and give the owner hands-on steps to verify the result.
6. Update `docs/progress.md`, record what was learned, and record significant decisions with a status of confirmed, proposed, or unresolved. Do not describe planned or unverified behaviour as working.
7. Pause when the agreed milestone is complete. Do not continue through the roadmap automatically.
8. Never commit credentials, tokens, signing keys, private configuration, or the owner's personal content.
9. Do not deploy, incur costs, enable paid services, or push changes to a remote without explicit permission.
10. Keep documentation consistent with behaviour that has actually been verified.
